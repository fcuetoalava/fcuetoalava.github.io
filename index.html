Francisco Cueto Alava

PhD Candidate in Economics, University of Sussex

I am an applied microeconomist working on the economics of crime, political economy and development economics. My research examines how crime, migration and the delivery of public policies shape citizens' trust in public institutions and political accountability, using quasi-experimental methods and linked survey and administrative data.

Before the PhD, I spent more than nine years designing and running impact evaluations for government ministries. I am currently a Research Lecturer in Economics at Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas (UPC) and a Doctoral Tutor at the University of Sussex.
