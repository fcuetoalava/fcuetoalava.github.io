Job Market Paper

Designed Nationally, Blamed Locally: Implementation Failure and Trust in Government in Peru

Single-authored · October 2026 draft

Abstract: When a nationally designed programme fails at the point of local delivery, which tier of government do citizens blame? I exploit a 2015 administrative failure that interrupted Peru's national school feeding programme, Qali Warma, in 22% of districts, where design and financing were national but procurement involved municipalities. Using difference-in-differences on matched districts, I find that trust in the district municipality fell by 5.8–6.4 percentage points (29–32%), while evaluations of the central government and the Ministry of Education were unchanged; electoral data suggest the penalty carried into the next municipal election. Decentralising implementation shifts the political risk of failure onto local governments.
